<objective>
Close the pairing loop with cryptographic verification: parse the pair-success container from adv.proto, verify its HMAC and account/device signatures fail-closed, persist the verified credential update in the fixed order, reply only after the write is observable, and clear credentials on terminal logout.

Purpose: without this, any forged or malformed pair-success notification could mint a Success event and poisoned credentials — the highest-severity trust boundary in the phase. It also completes CONN-03's durability story for the pairing write path.
Output: PairSuccessVerifier, adv.proto, ordered coordinator success handling, credential clearing on logout, golden pair-success fixture, ConnectionCredentialTests logout coverage.
</objective>
