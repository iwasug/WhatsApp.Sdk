## Trust Boundaries

| Boundary | Description |
|----------|-------------|
| CDN bytes → verify pipeline | Untrusted download bytes cross into the triple-gate verify here; expiry statuses cross into the retry trigger here |
| Peer rmr notification → re-download | Adversary-influenced fresh paths and result codes cross into host/peer selection here |
| Retry tracker → wire | Self-inflicted request volume crosses into protocol egress here; the cap bounds it |

## STRIDE Threat Register

| Threat ID | Category | Component | Severity | Disposition | Mitigation Plan |
|-----------|----------|-----------|----------|-------------|-----------------|
| T-06-03 | Denial of Service | Media-retry requests (retry storms re-uploading or re-fetching without bound; host-loopable recovery) | medium | mitigate | At most three requests per message id from a bounded process-local tracker with hourly eviction; recovery is protocol egress not host-loopable; cap exhaustion surfaces one typed outcome and stops; error-code terminals respected |
| T-06-06 | Denial of Service | Oversize media (RAM blowup from unbounded buffering; pipe stalls from slow hosts) | medium | mitigate | Byte caps enforced pre-upload and pre-decrypt; Content-Length checked against fileLength; temp-file-backed streaming with bounded Channels-style backpressure; host owns disposal with a documented cleanup bound |
</threat_model>
